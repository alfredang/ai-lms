Learner Guide

Learn how to get the most out of LearnHub as a learner - from enrolling in courses to earning badges and using the AI assistant.

Demo Account

Try the learner experience: learner@learnhub.com / admin123

Your Dashboard

The Learner Dashboard is your home base in LearnHub. Here's what you'll find:

Enrolled Courses

View all your current course enrollments and continue where you left off.

Progress Overview

See your learning statistics, completion rates, and recent activity.

XP & Level

Track your experience points and see how close you are to leveling up.

Badges

View your earned badges and discover new ones to unlock.

Enrolling in Courses

Follow these steps to enroll in a new course:

1

Browse Courses

Click "Courses" in the navigation to view all available courses. Use filters to narrow down by category or difficulty level.

2

View Course Details

Click on a course card to see the full description, curriculum, instructor info, and delivery format (Instructor-Led or E-Learning).

3

Select a Class

Choose an available class cohort. Each class has its own start/end dates and trainer.

4

Enroll

Click the "Enroll Now" button. You'll receive 25 XP for enrolling and can start learning immediately!

Tracking Your Progress

LearnHub helps you stay on track with comprehensive progress tracking:

Course Progress

Each enrolled course shows a progress bar indicating how many lessons you've completed. Click on a course to see detailed lesson-by-lesson progress.

Learning Streaks

Keep your streak alive by logging in and completing at least one activity daily. Streaks unlock special badges and bonus XP:

  • 7-day streak: 100 XP bonus
  • 30-day streak: 500 XP bonus
  • Special streak badges available

XP & Badges

LearnHub makes learning rewarding with a comprehensive gamification system.

Earning XP

Action XP Earned
Complete a lesson 50 XP
Submit an assignment 25 XP
Pass assignment (>70%) 100 XP
Perfect score (100%) 200 XP
Daily login 10 XP
Enroll in a course 25 XP

Level Progression

As you earn XP, you'll level up and unlock new titles:

1 Novice (0 XP)
2 Beginner (500 XP)
3 Apprentice (1,500 XP)
4 Intermediate (3,500 XP)
5 Advanced (7,000 XP)
6 Expert (12,000 XP)

AI Learning Assistant

LearnHub features an AI-powered learning assistant available on every page. Look for the chat icon in the bottom-right corner.

What You Can Ask

  • Explain concepts from your current lesson
  • Get help understanding difficult topics
  • Ask questions about course materials
  • Request practice questions or summaries
  • Get personalized learning recommendations
Context-Aware

The AI assistant knows which course and lesson you're viewing, so responses are tailored to your current learning context.
