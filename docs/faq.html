Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about LearnHub.

General

LearnHub is an AI-powered Learning Management System (LMS) designed to make learning engaging and effective. It features AI-assisted learning, gamification with XP and badges, role-based access control, and support for multiple course delivery formats.
LearnHub offers both free and paid courses. Many courses are available at no cost, while premium courses may require payment. The demo version at ai-lms-xi.vercel.app is fully free to explore with demo accounts.
LearnHub is built with Next.js 14 (App Router), React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL with Prisma ORM, NextAuth for authentication, and integrates with multiple AI providers including OpenAI, Google Gemini, and Anthropic Claude.

Account

Click "Get Started" on the homepage, then fill in your name, email, and password. You can also sign up using Google or GitHub OAuth. New accounts are automatically assigned the Learner role.
LearnHub has four roles: Learner (enroll in courses, earn XP), Trainer (manage classes, grade assignments), Admin (manage users and courses), and Superadmin (full system access, AI settings, API keys).
Click the sun/moon icon in the header to toggle between themes. You can choose Light, Dark, or System (which follows your device preferences). Your choice is saved automatically.
Role changes can only be made by Admins or Superadmins. If you need a different role, contact your organization's administrator.

Courses

Browse the course catalog, click on a course to view details, select an available class, and click "Enroll Now". You'll earn 25 XP upon enrollment and can start learning immediately.
Instructor-Led courses have live sessions with a trainer at scheduled times. E-Learning courses are self-paced with pre-recorded content you can access anytime.
Classroom sessions take place at a physical location. Virtual sessions are conducted online via video conferencing, allowing you to attend from anywhere.
You earn XP by completing lessons (50 XP), submitting assignments (25 XP), achieving high scores (100-200 XP), maintaining daily login streaks, and more. Badges are unlocked automatically as you reach milestones.

Technical

The AI assistant uses context-aware prompts that include information about your current course and lesson. It connects to configured AI providers (like OpenAI, Gemini, or Claude) to generate helpful responses tailored to your learning context.
LearnHub works best on modern browsers including Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge. We recommend keeping your browser updated to the latest version for the best experience.
Yes! LearnHub is fully responsive and works on smartphones and tablets. The interface adapts to different screen sizes for comfortable viewing and navigation.
LearnHub is open source! Clone the repository from GitHub, set up a PostgreSQL database (Neon recommended), configure your environment variables, and deploy to Vercel or any Node.js hosting platform. See the README for detailed setup instructions.
