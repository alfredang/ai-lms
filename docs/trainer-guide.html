Trainer Guide

Learn how to manage classes, grade assignments, and track student progress as a trainer.

Demo Account

Try the trainer experience: trainer@learnhub.com / admin123

Your Dashboard

The Trainer Dashboard gives you an overview of your teaching activities:

Active Classes

View and manage all classes you're currently teaching.

Pending Submissions

See assignments waiting for your review and grading.

Student Progress

Track overall progress across all your students.

Schedule

View upcoming class sessions and deadlines.

Managing Classes

Each course can have multiple class cohorts. As a trainer, you can:

  • View enrolled students and their contact information
  • Create and manage lessons within your classes
  • Upload course materials (videos, PDFs, documents)
  • Set assignment due dates
  • Track overall class progress

Grading Assignments

Review and grade student submissions:

1

Review Submissions

Access pending submissions from your dashboard or the Grading section.

2

Evaluate Work

Review the student's submission content and any attached files.

3

Assign Score

Enter a score (0-100) based on the assignment rubric.

4

Provide Feedback

Add constructive feedback to help students improve.

AI Assistant for Trainers

The AI assistant can help trainers with:

  • Generating quiz questions for lessons
  • Creating grading rubrics
  • Drafting feedback templates
  • Identifying common student struggles
  • Suggesting lesson improvements
